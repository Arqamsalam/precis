      How Précis works

      A précis (say “pray-see”) is a summary that keeps the original's voice. Think of Précis as a skilled editor making an abridged edition: the book gets shorter, but it's still the same book, with the same characters, arguments, tone and even its uncomfortable parts.

      The length slider

      The percentage is how long the summary is compared with the original. A 20,000-word text at 25% becomes about 5,000 words. Low percentages keep the skeleton (every major event and argument); higher ones put back the muscle and texture (details, examples, dialogue, quotes).

      Long documents

      Very long texts are handled like a careful reader with a notebook: Précis reads them in parts and condenses each part to the same percentage, while remembering what it has already written so the result flows as one piece. For very short summaries of huge books, it first takes detailed notes on each part, then writes the final summary from all the notes.

      Nothing left out

      The AI is instructed to keep everything that is in your text, including mature, explicit, violent or controversial material, in proportion and in the original's own words and tone, without warnings or moralizing. Choose “neutral wording” if you want such passages kept but described plainly.

      Engines

      • Claude AI (recommended): best quality. Needs an Anthropic API key; a typical article costs well under one cent with Haiku and a few cents with Opus.
      • Other AI / local: any OpenAI-compatible service, or a model running on your own computer for complete privacy.
      • Offline: free, instant, private; picks the author's most important sentences.

      Tips

      • You can edit the extracted text before summarizing, for example to remove an index or bibliography.
      • For a PDF, choose a page range to summarize just one chapter.
      • Use Custom instructions to focus on something specific.
      • Press Ctrl + Enter to summarize. Every result is saved in History on this device.

      Privacy

      Files are opened inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded to this website. When you summarize, the text is sent straight from your browser to the AI service you chose. Offline mode sends nothing at all.
